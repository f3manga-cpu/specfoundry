# AI Agent Architecture Readiness Review

**A fixed-scope technical review for founders and engineering teams preparing an AI agent to take real actions.**

**Indicative fee: €1,490 excl. applicable taxes, for one defined workflow.** Final scope, price, schedule, and payment terms must be confirmed in writing before any work begins. No payment is collected through this repository.

## What you receive

1. **Action and spend boundary map** — what the agent can observe, decide and execute; who approves costly or irreversible actions; failure and rollback paths.
2. **Evidence-backed readiness matrix** — critical tests for permissions, idempotency, audit trail, retries, cost caps, data exposure, and external outcome verification. Each finding is marked verified, unverified, or not applicable, with evidence references.
3. **Prioritized implementation brief** — top risks, minimal changes to unblock launch, acceptance tests, and a sequenced plan for the next engineering sprint.

**Delivery target:** five business days after a mutually agreed start date and receipt of sufficient non-sensitive technical documentation. A review may conclude that the workflow is not ready; this is not a certification or a promise of production safety.

## Who this is for

- Teams building agents that call paid APIs, trigger payments, send messages, modify customer data, or run unattended.
- Founders with a prototype who need an independent specification and a practical verification plan before granting more autonomy.
- Engineering managers who want to distinguish observed controls from architectural assumptions.

## Scope and exclusions

One workflow, up to three external integrations, a short architecture walkthrough, and a written report. The initial review uses architecture diagrams, redacted logs, schemas and test evidence provided by the team. **Never post API keys, personal data, private customer records or security-sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.** Any deeper implementation, security testing, legal/compliance opinion, or production access requires a separate agreement.

No claim of SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, EU AI Act, financial-services compliance or penetration-test certification is made.
